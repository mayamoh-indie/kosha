Ontario · 2026 estimates

01 / YOUR PAYCHEQUE

What could you take home?

An annualized payroll estimate for a full-year Ontario employee aged 18–64, with one job and basic personal credits.

Salary assumptions & sources

Ontario residence and employment throughout 2026. All earnings are pensionable and insurable. Includes federal and Ontario basic credits, Canada employment amount, base CPP/EI credits, enhanced CPP deductions, Ontario surtax, basic tax reduction, and health premium.

Does not include RRSP deductions, employer benefits, pension contributions, other income, dependants, or year-end credits such as LIFT. Actual paycheques vary as CPP/EI maximums are reached. Period amounts divide the annual estimate; they are not a payroll schedule or a tax-return calculation.

02 / YOUR RRSP

Know what reaches your account.

Ordinary taxable RRSP withdrawals for Ontario residents. Withholding is an advance tax payment, not your final tax bill.

Withholding on the whole withdrawal

Up to $5,000 10%

Over $5,000 through $15,000 20%

Over $15,000 30%

Withdrawal assumptions & sources

The rate applies to the entire gross amount, not only the portion above a threshold. Target-net mode finds the smallest gross amount, in cents, that provides at least your target after withholding. Crossing a threshold can reduce net proceeds.

Excludes institution fees, Quebec, non-residents, spousal attribution, RRIF minimums, and eligible Home Buyers’ Plan or Lifelong Learning Plan withdrawals. Final income tax depends on your full-year circumstances and is not calculated here. Instalments from one withdrawal request may be assessed together.
