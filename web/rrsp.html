Withdrawal assumptions & sources

The rate applies to the entire gross amount, not only the portion above a threshold. Target-net mode finds the smallest gross amount, in cents, that provides at least your target after withholding. Crossing a threshold can reduce net proceeds.

Excludes institution fees, Quebec, non-residents, spousal attribution, RRIF minimums, and eligible Home Buyers’ Plan or Lifelong Learning Plan withdrawals. Final income tax depends on your full-year circumstances and is not calculated here. Instalments from one withdrawal request may be assessed together.
