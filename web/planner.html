How these estimates work

Fixed annual rates divided by 12; interest is charged before monthly payments and rounded to cents. Minimum payments stay fixed, capped by the amount owed. Paid-off payments roll into your constant monthly budget. No new borrowing, fees, or rate changes. Equal priorities follow your entered order. Month 1 is the first simulated payment cycle, not a calendar date. Plans stop after 1,200 months if unfinished.

This prototype does not connect to banks or save your entries. Refreshing reloads fictional sample data. Calculations run on your computer. This is a planning estimate, not a lender statement.
