Calculation assumptions and limits

All amounts are CAD. This general scenario does not verify RRSP, TFSA or FHSA contribution room. Starting balances and contributions are separate from your saved accounts and goals; nothing is saved or moved.

The annual fee is subtracted from the nominal annual return. The tax assumption then reduces positive after-fee returns before compounding; negative returns receive no tax credit. A zero tax input shows growth without tax drag. This is a simplified interest-like scenario, not a Canadian tax calculation: dividends, capital gains, deductions and withdrawal taxes are not modelled.

The annual nominal rate is divided by compounding periods. Equivalent growth per contribution period is (1 + net annual rate / compounding periods) raised to (compounding periods / contribution periods), minus 1. This uses evenly spaced periods, not actual calendar dates or institution-specific interest-crediting rules. Contributions occur at the selected beginning or end of each period.

When enabled, contributions rise once per year, after the first year, by the inflation assumption and are rounded to cents. Returns retain calculation precision until display. Today’s dollars divide each year’s balance by cumulative inflation. Returns are constant hypothetical assumptions; losses are possible and results are not guaranteed.

Feature references: Fidelity investment growth calculator and Dynamic savings calculator. Kosha uses its own documented model; results may differ.
