Assumptions and contribution room

End-of-month contributions. Monthly growth rate = (annual nominal return minus annual fee) / 12. Constant assumptions, no refund reinvestment or withdrawals. Balances are before withdrawal tax, with a separate inflation-adjusted result. Returns are scenarios and are not guaranteed.

The optional room check covers only the next 12 months (or the shorter projection), not calendar-year contribution timing. It does not calculate your personal room, deduction eligibility or future room. Use CRA available contribution room adjusted for recent deposits, rather than assuming the deduction limit is identical. Future contributions require future room.
