# Plan a yearly bill

Use this for a known future charge such as insurance, property tax or a membership. Kosha treats the bill as a savings goal, with a due date and money earmarked toward it.

## Create a bill

1. Open **Money overview**.
2. Under **Add or edit an entry**, set **Entry type** to **Savings goal**.
3. Enter a name, the **Target or due date**, and the **Target or bill amount (CAD)**.
4. Enter the **Amount earmarked (CAD)** you have already set aside.
5. Check **This is a yearly or irregular bill** and choose **Save entry**.

The bill appears under **Savings goals and bills**, with a progress bar and an estimate of what to reserve each month.

## Example

For CAD 1,200 of car insurance due six calendar months from now, with CAD 300 already earmarked, the remaining CAD 900 requires about **CAD 150 per month**. Kosha divides the remaining amount across the calendar-month difference and rounds up to the next cent.

A **Due soon** label appears within 30 days, including the due date. A passed date shows **Past due date**. If the target is in this calendar month or earlier, the row shows the full amount still needed instead of spreading it across months. A fully funded bill can still be due soon. These are on-page indicators, not email or background notifications.

## Update or pay the bill

Choose **Edit** to update the amount, earmark or due date. Choose **Mark paid** only after you have paid it yourself. Review the confirmation: accepting moves the due date one year later and resets the earmark to zero. Cancelling leaves it unchanged. A February 29 date advances to February 28 in a non-leap year.

Payment does not log an expense, withdraw money, update an account balance or send a payment. Record actual spending separately if you want it included in the expense log, and update account snapshots from your records. Earmarked money never adds to your assets again.

## Current limits

The reset always advances one year, including for bills labelled irregular. Edit the next due date yourself for a different schedule. If the bill is several years overdue, one reset may leave it overdue. Due dates and saved money remain in this browser only.
