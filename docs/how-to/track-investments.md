# Track investment accounts

1. Open **Home** and choose **Asset account** under **Add or edit an entry**.
2. Name the account, for example “Retirement TFSA”. Choose its account type.
3. Enter its current CAD balance and the **Balance as of** date from your records.
4. Optionally enter **Total contributions to this account**. Use your own reference total as of that balance date. Leave it blank if unknown; enter zero only when you mean zero.
5. Choose **Save entry**. Use **Edit** on the same account when updating its balance or contribution total.

**Registered investments** groups TFSA, RRSP, FHSA, RESP, RRIF and LIRA accounts. **Non-registered investments** groups the explicit Non-registered type and older Investments entries. Older Investments entries retain their original type and display a reminder to confirm it using Edit. Cash and other assets are listed separately.

Each investment group shows its balance total. Each account shows its balance date and recorded contributions, or “not recorded”. All account balances count once toward assets and net worth. Contribution totals do not add to assets or affect income, expenses or accessible cash. You may enter contributions greater than the current balance; losses and withdrawals can make that legitimate.

## Log contributions and withdrawals

1. First save an investment account. Then choose **Investment contribution / withdrawal** as the entry type.
2. Give the activity a description in **Name**, select its investment account, and choose Contribution or Withdrawal.
3. Enter the activity date and a positive CAD amount, then save.
4. The **Investment activity** section shows entries for the reporting month. Change that month to see older entries. Use **Edit** or **Delete** on a log entry to correct it.

Account rows show all logged contributions, withdrawals and their difference, even when another reporting month is selected. These are totals of the entries you logged, not lifetime totals unless your log is complete. The manual contribution total is a separate reference figure; logs are never added to it. Logging activity does not update account balances, accessible cash, income or expenses. Update the dated balance from your statement separately. A withdrawal larger than logged contributions is allowed because activity before tracking began may be missing.

An account with activity cannot be deleted or changed into a non-investment asset until you delete or reassign its linked activity. Editing an activity's account moves it between logs, without moving real money.

This version keeps a dated contribution/withdrawal log, manual balance snapshots and reference contribution totals. It does not keep balance history, calculate investment returns or tax cost bases, determine contribution room, or distinguish personal contributions from transfers and employer contributions. Do not infer a return from balance minus contributions or net logged flows. Account types organize your records; they do not establish eligibility or tax treatment.

Accounts stay in this browser only. Existing accounts need no migration, and an absent contribution total is never filled with a made-up zero. Updating an account replaces its previous snapshot. Switching an investment to a cash or other-asset type requires clearing its contribution total explicitly; keep a separate copy if you need that reference.

Use [Savings growth](project-savings.md) to explore future scenarios separately. Account balances are not automatically passed into the calculator.
